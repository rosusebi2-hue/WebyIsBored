BEFORE YOU JUMP IN

Stick & Swing

Learn the controls in a short playable tutorial, then fight through four rooms and one boss. Your progress is saved at room entrances.

Move
WASDor arrow keys
Aim
Move your mouse; Space uses aim assist
Swing
Hold left click or Space
Block
Hold right click or F
Dash
Shift
Pause
Esc

Face the danger. Hold guard to block from the front, or tap it just before impact for a perfect block. Perfect blocks reflect shots and power your next swing.

On a touchscreen, drag the left stick to move. Hold Swing or Guard and tap Dash on the right. Aim assist selects the nearest enemy.
