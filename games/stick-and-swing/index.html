How the game works

Combat

Left Click / Space: swing your sword toward the cursor. Shift: dash. Movement and positioning matter more than standing still and trading hits.

Blocking

Hold Right Click: raise your shield. You cannot attack while blocking. Only attacks inside your shield’s frontal angle are stopped, and blocking consumes shield energy.

Gold & Shop

Enemies drop gold, but every completed combat wave also gives a guaranteed clear reward. Stats are simple upgrades. Perks add mechanics. Swords, shields and clothes are build-defining equipment.

Ink Levels

Kills fill the Ink bar. Leveling pauses the game and gives three randomized perks. These are separate from the gold shop, so a run grows through both money and combat experience.

Relics

Relics are rare run modifiers found from elites, treasure, bosses and events. You can carry two. If you take a third, the oldest relic is replaced.

Story vs Wave Mode

Story Mode uses animated comic cinematics and autosaves your scene, build, HP, equipment and in-progress battle. Wave Mode is the branching replayable mode where you choose what kind of page comes next.
